01 About

A contemporary Indian composer and music producer from Thrissur, Kerala — and proudly a blind musical artist.

His work moves between energetic electronic arrangements and melody-led compositions, with each release shaped around a distinct musical idea.

His original singles are released across Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music and SoundCloud, and he is listed on MusicBrainz, Discogs and Google’s Knowledge Panel.
